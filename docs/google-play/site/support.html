Tiltling Support

Tiltling is a tilt-controlled arcade game by Fredrik Johansson (Johansson Interactive).

How to play

Tilt your phone to steer your dot. Catch small dots of the same colour and avoid the others. Catch a large, pulsing point dot to change colour and earn extra score. Make catches close together to build a combo.

Controls and sound

Use the pause settings to adjust tilt sensitivity, calibrate the level position, or change music and sound settings. The game is designed for portrait play with motion sensors. Its music can stay silent when other audio is playing.

Scores and settings

Your best score and settings are stored locally. There is no account or online leaderboard. Clearing the app’s storage resets your saved data. Device backups may restore previous data.

Contact

Email support@johanssoninteractive.com. For a problem report, include your device model, Android version, Tiltling version and the steps that caused the problem. Only include screenshots or other details you are comfortable sharing.
