Tiltling Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

Tiltling is a single-player tilt-controlled game provided by Fredrik Johansson (Johansson Interactive). This policy explains how the game handles information. For privacy questions, contact support@johanssoninteractive.com.

Information used on your device

Tiltling uses your device’s motion and orientation sensors to control the game. These readings are processed on your device and are not sent to us. The game does not store a history of your movements.

The game saves your best score and preferences in its local app storage. Preferences include sound and music settings, tilt sensitivity and calibrated neutral positions. These values let the game remember your choices between sessions.

The game can check whether other audio is playing so that its music can respond appropriately. This check provides an audio-playing status; it does not record audio or read track names, audio files or your listening history. Tiltling plays bundled music and effects and does not record your microphone.

Collection and sharing

Tiltling does not require an account and does not send gameplay data, scores, settings or sensor readings to servers operated by us. The game contains no advertising, analytics or third-party crash-reporting integration, and no in-app purchases. We do not sell your information or share gameplay information with advertisers.

Storage, security and deletion

Scores and preferences are kept in the app’s storage, subject to your device’s operating-system protections. They remain until the app’s data is cleared or removed. On Android, you can clear them in Settings → Apps → Tiltling → Storage → Clear storage (the wording may vary). Uninstalling normally removes local app data.

Your operating system may back up or transfer app data according to your device settings. Backed-up scores and preferences may be restored when you reinstall the game or move to another device. Manage and delete those backups through your device or backup provider. We do not operate or have access to your device backup.

If you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request and resolve problems. Our email provider processes these messages on our behalf. Access is limited to those handling support. Please do not include sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request.

We delete support correspondence within 12 months after the request is resolved, unless a longer period is required by law or needed for an ongoing dispute. You can ask us to delete your correspondence by contacting the address above.

Children

The game’s local data handling is the same for all players. It does not ask players for their name, age or contact details. If a child has sent personal information to us through support, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its deletion.

Platform and website services

Google Play, your operating system and your backup provider may process information independently under their own privacy policies, including store transactions and platform diagnostics. When you visit this policy or our support website, the hosting provider may process ordinary request information such as your IP address for site delivery and security. These pages do not include our own analytics or advertising scripts.

Changes and contact

We will publish updates to this policy here and change the effective date when our practices change. Contact Fredrik Johansson (Johansson Interactive) at support@johanssoninteractive.com with questions or requests about information we hold.
